Shishir Kolathaya is an Associate Professor at the Centre for Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) and the Department of Computer Science and Automation (CSA) at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru. He received his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering in 2016 from the Georgia Institute of Technology, his M.S. in Electrical Engineering in 2012 from Texas A&M University, and his B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering in 2008 from the National Institute of Technology Karnataka, Surathkal. He worked as a postdoctoral scholar at the California Institute of Technology in 2017 and served as an INSPIRE Faculty Fellow from 2018 to 2020.
 
Shishir leads the Stochastic Robotics Lab at IISc, where his research focuses on legged robotics, safety-critical control, and machine learning for robotic systems. His work combines theoretical advances in control and reinforcement learning with the development and testing of quadrupedal robots capable of navigating challenging terrains.
 
Beyond academia, Shishir is the co-founder of Strider Robotics, a Bengaluru-based deep-tech startup that emerged from research on quadrupedal robots at IISc. Co-founded with Aditya Varma Sagi, the company aims to develop indigenous autonomous quadrupedal robots for industrial inspection, monitoring, and logistics. With a focus on locally developed hardware, intelligent locomotion, and autonomous navigation, Strider Robotics seeks to translate advanced academic research into practical robotic solutions for industry. Shishir contributes his expertise in robotics and control systems to help bridge the gap between laboratory research and real-world industrial applications.
